# Kyoto — 4-Day Itinerary

### Priority key

* ⭐ **Must see**
* 🟢 **High priority**
* 🟡 **Optional — do if you have time/energy**

---

## Day 1 — Arashiyama & Saga

**Morning**

* ⭐ **Arashiyama Bamboo Forest** — take your time; this is a major part of the day
* 🟢 **Okochi Sanso**
* ⭐ **Tenryu-ji**

**Afternoon**

* 🟢 **Saga Toriimoto Preserved Street**
* 🟢 **Nisonin Temple**
* 🟢 **Arashiyama Monkey Park Iwatayama**

**Late afternoon / Evening**

* ⭐ **Togetsukyo Bridge**
* 🟡 **Kimono Forest**
* 🟡 **Fukushichi** — snack/dessert stop

**Day philosophy:** Don't try to maximize this day. The bamboo forest + Saga area should feel like a long, leisurely walk rather than a checklist.

---

## Day 2 — Higashiyama, Gion & Pontocho

**Morning**

* ⭐ **Kiyomizu-dera**
* ⭐ **Sannenzaka / Ninenzaka**
* 🟢 **Kodai-ji**

**Afternoon**

* ⭐ **Yasaka Shrine**
* ⭐ **Gion**
* 🟡 **Chionin**
* 🟢 **Kamo River** — walk toward central Kyoto

**Late afternoon / Evening**

* 🟢 **Nishiki Market**
* 🟡 **Kimono Tea Ceremony MAIKOYA Nishiki** — if you want the experience
* ⭐ **Pontocho**
* 🟢 **Kyo-Yakiniku HIRO Pontocho Annex** — dinner

**Day philosophy:** This is primarily a **walking/atmosphere day**. Don't rush from attraction to attraction.

---

## Day 3 — Northern Kyoto & Central Kyoto

**Morning**

* ⭐ **Kinkaku-ji**
* ⭐ **Ryoan-ji**
* 🟢 **Ninna-ji**

**Afternoon**

* ⭐ **Kyoto Imperial Palace**
* 🟢 **Kyoto Gyoen National Garden**
* 🟡 **Daitoku-ji / Ryogen-in**

**Late afternoon / Evening**

* 🟢 **Nijō Castle** — optional depending on energy

**Day philosophy:** Kinkaku-ji and Ryoan-ji are the priorities. If you've had enough temples by the afternoon, skip the optional stops rather than forcing them.

---

## Day 4 — Fushimi Inari & Eastern Kyoto

**Early Morning**

* ⭐ **Fushimi Inari Taisha** — allow plenty of time to walk up through the torii gates

**Late Morning**

* 🟢 **Tofuku-ji**

**Afternoon**

* ⭐ **Tetsugaku no Michi / Philosopher's Path**
* 🟢 **Nanzen-ji**
* 🟢 **Eikando**

**Late afternoon**

* 🟡 **Shinshō Gokurakuji (Shinnyodō)**

**Day philosophy:** Fushimi Inari deserves a proper visit rather than a quick photo stop. In the afternoon, enjoy the Philosopher's Path as a walk and treat the temples around it as optional additions.

---

# Intentionally Left Out

These aren't necessarily bad attractions — they're simply the ones I'd sacrifice to keep a **4-day Kyoto trip relaxed**.

* 🔴 **Kyoto Railway Museum** — intentionally skipped
* 🔴 **Uji** — save for a 5-day version
* 🔴 **Tenzan no Yu** — better as a relaxation/flex option
* 🔴 **To-ji Temple** — interesting but not essential
* 🟡 **Daitoku-ji / Ryogen-in** — optional on Day 3
* 🟡 **Chionin** — optional on Day 2
* 🟡 **Shinnyodō** — optional on Day 4
* 🟡 **Nijō Castle** — optional on Day 3
* 🟡 **Kimono Forest** — easy to skip
* 🟡 **MAIKOYA Tea Ceremony** — optional experience rather than sightseeing
* 🟡 **Fukushichi** — food stop rather than a destination
* 🟡 **Onya Kede** — optional food stop
* 🟡 **Pontocho Park** — don't treat it as a separate attraction; you'll experience the area while exploring Pontocho/Kamo River
