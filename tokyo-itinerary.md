# 🇯🇵 6-Day Tokyo + Kawaguchiko Itinerary

> **Strategy:** 5 focused Tokyo days + 1 night in Kawaguchiko/Fuji.
> Keep optional attractions flexible so the trip doesn't feel like a checklist.

---

## Day 1 — West Tokyo: Harajuku → Shibuya → Shinjuku

### Morning — Harajuku

* ⭐ Meiji Jingu
* ⭐ Harajuku
* ⭐ Takeshita Street

### Afternoon — Omotesando → Shibuya

* 🟢 Omotesando
* ⭐ Hachiko Statue
* ⭐ Shibuya Crossing
* Explore Shibuya

### Sunset

* ⭐ Shibuya Sky

### Evening — Shinjuku

* ⭐ Shinjuku
* ⭐ Kabukicho
* ⭐ Omoide Yokocho
* 🟢 Godzilla Head
* 🟢 Giant 3D Cat

### Optional

* 🟡 Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building

  * Skip if doing Shibuya Sky; there is no need for another observation deck.

---

## Day 2 — Old Tokyo: Asakusa → Skytree → Ueno → Akihabara

### Morning — Asakusa

* ⭐ Asakusa
* ⭐ Senso-ji
* ⭐ Nakamise Shopping Street

### Afternoon

* ⭐ Tokyo Skytree
* ⭐ Ueno Park
* ⭐ Ameyoko

### Evening

* ⭐ Akihabara

### Optional

* ⭐ Imperial Palace
* Explore the East Gardens

### Afternoon — Tokyo Station / Marunouchi

* ⭐ Marunouchi
* ⭐ Tokyo Station
* ⭐ Tokyo Character Street

### Late Afternoon / Evening — Ginza

* ⭐ Ginza

* ⭐ Morton's Steakhouse Marunouchi

### Optional

* 🟡 Art Aquarium Museum

  * Skip if the day is getting too busy.

### Morning

* ⭐ teamLab

### Afternoon

* 🟡 Odaiba
* Odaiba waterfront

### Evening

* ⭐ Tokyo Tower

## Day 5 — Quieter Tokyo: Tetsugakudo → Fukagawa → Ebisu

### Morning

* ⭐ Tetsugakudo Park

### Late Morning — Nakano

* 🟡 Nakano
* 🟡 Nakano Broadway

### Afternoon

* ⭐ Fukagawa Edo Museum

### Evening — Ebisu

* ⭐ Afuri Ebisu
* ⭐ Matsugen Ebisu Store
* Explore Ebisu

### Optional

* 🟡 Nakano / Nakano Broadway

  * Skip if you want a slower day.

---

## Day 6 — Kawaguchiko / Mt. Fuji

### Morning

* Tokyo → Kawaguchiko
* Arrive around late morning if possible
* Leave luggage at hotel/ryokan

### Afternoon

Choose 2–3 depending on weather and energy:

* ⭐ Lake Kawaguchi
* ⭐ Oishi Park
* ⭐ Mt. Fuji viewpoints
* 🟢 Mt. Fuji Panoramic Ropeway
* 🟢 Lake cruise
* 🟢 Chureito Pagoda
* 🟡 Other Fuji Five Lakes attractions

### Evening

* ⭐ Stay overnight in Kawaguchiko
* ⭐ Onsen / ryokan
* Dinner
* Enjoy the Fuji area after the day-trippers leave

### Day 7 Morning

* ⭐ Check Mt. Fuji visibility early in the morning
* Enjoy another viewpoint if weather is good
* Return to Tokyo late morning / early afternoon

* Nakano Broadway while visiting Tetsugakudo

# 🟡 Optional — Skip If Tired

These are worth keeping on the master list, but **don't treat them as mandatory stops**.

* Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
* Odaiba
* Art Aquarium Museum
* Ueno Park
* Nezu Shrine
* Nakano Broadway

### Priority for cutting

If a day starts feeling too busy, remove things in roughly this order:

1. 🔴 Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
2. 🔴 Odaiba
3. 🔴 Art Aquarium Museum
4. 🔴 Nakano Broadway
5. 🔴 Ueno Park
6. 🔴 Nezu Shrine
